Slingshot

Game Rules

This is a simple slingshot game.

  • Hit the octagons with the ball to earn points.
  • Click, drag, and release to launch the ball.
  • The round will last 60 seconds.
  • Click the button to reset.
  • Unless you hit a target, the balls will stay in the game, so have fun with the physics. Or don't.
